# Site spec

Written by the Analyst from: CV and LinkedIn text. Revised 2026-10-13 against my own career record, which is the authority for every claim on the site.

## Who
Bruno Manfredi, Solutions Architect & Technical Account Manager.

## Goal
Help find a full-time job: Solutions Architect, Technical Account Manager or engineering leadership, available now.

## For whom
(a guess) Hiring managers, CTOs, VP of Engineering, and technical recruiters seeking senior solutions architecture, technical discovery, or engineering leadership talent.

## What a visitor should do
Write to me by email or connect on LinkedIn about a full-time role, and read the Artemis Quiver code on GitHub as evidence of hands-on work.

## Language
English (en).

## Sections
- First screen: photo, pitch, 3 concrete highlights, availability (full-time roles; remote across Europe or hybrid in Milan or Turin; EU citizen, no sponsorship required) with an email action, Manta, Italy, and languages: English and Spanish native, Italian at professional working proficiency.
- Bio: 4 short paragraphs: the career from 2011, the Toptal client lifecycle, AI building and advisory since 2026, where I live.
- Projects: Artemis Quiver as a case study with a screenshot and GitHub link; Artemis Flow and the local LLM infrastructure, both private.
- Links: GitHub, LinkedIn and email.
- CV: experience (independent advisory, Toptal, BBVA, Globant, Atos), education exactly as conferred or attended, and hands-on skills only.
- Events: DevFest Milano 2026, workshop attendee.
- Now: job search, the Quiver application-assist panel, small-model scoring tests for Flow. Dated October 2026; update it monthly.
- Writing: two LinkedIn posts (9 October and 8 September 2026).

## Left out on purpose
- Phone number, postal address and VAT number: the site is for a job search, not for selling services.
- Client names: kept confidential.
- Skills I have assessed for clients but not used hands-on: they would need their own clearly labelled list, never a merged one.
- Earlier jobs from 2011 to 2013: not needed for the roles I am targeting.
